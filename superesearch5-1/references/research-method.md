# Research method

Use this skill for research planning, source selection, gap reviews and analysis within this repository. Load the selected exploration's brief, relevant phase entry and applicable local skills. Follow the host project's contribution instructions, when supplied for records and collaboration. Geography, technical constraints, acquisition preferences and domain inference rules belong in the exploration.

## Existing projects

Only when the selected exploration already exists, follow the host project's lifecycle protocol, when supplied: read its lifecycle, current brief, refinement record and latest answer/review before choosing work. If refinement is complete, use the saved corpus for the user's chosen action: more sources, an answer or result exploration. A bare "continue" gets a short recap and 2–3 suggested next actions; do not repeat introductory questions or start another round on your own. Reconcile stale or missing state from saved work before asking anything.

A new project has no menu: it runs from refinement into research. Update the lifecycle and generated overview after the user has seen the step's output, not before showing it.

## Establish the decision

Extract from the brief and latest user direction:

- The intended decision, unit of analysis and concrete output: a candidate, explanation, forecast, comparison or acquisition design.
- The present stage and evidence sufficient for its next action. Screening for paid verification requires a different standard from committing capital or asserting a validated prediction.
- Required parameters, exclusions, cost/time constraints, acquisition preferences and unresolved assumptions.
- What constitutes a useful first example, repeatable production and eventual completion. Keep these separate from source-count growth.

Infer and document provisional sufficiency criteria from the intended decision, stakes and available context; do not require the user to define them before research starts. Explain why they are appropriate and let the user edit them later. Mark inferred criteria separately from explicit user requirements.

Keep this contract compact in the existing brief or working review. Revise it when steering changes the objective. If a missing parameter does not block useful work, state a provisional assumption and proceed. Do not import an example's constraints into a different brief.

## Discover broadly, then focus

Start with the saved register and observations. Map material evidence needs to source roles and missing fields, joins or acquisition routes. In the initial pass, explore complementary avenues:

1. **Direct evidence:** datasets, registries or records that identify the target or answer the decision directly.
2. **Decomposition:** component datasets, rules, constraints and model inputs that can be combined to answer it. Specify the proposed join or inference and the weakest link.
3. **Comparable cases and adjacent opportunities:** precedents, proxies, alternative target types and evidence holders that could change the approach.

Seek useful breadth before concentrating on the first convenient route. Retain complementary evidence and promising hard-to-access sources; avoid collecting material without a stated role. Bound ancillary plausibility checks so they do not become a second research project. Explore new routes when evidence reveals a consequential gap.

Public, proprietary, permissioned, paid, physical, institutional, expert and unknown-access sources are eligible. Access difficulty affects acquisition order, not intrinsic usefulness. An exploration's public-first preference can govern execution while the design still presents the wider options.

Use the [source triage workflow](source-triage.md) and existing probe/recording tools. Inspect deeply when the next check could change the decision, establish a useful field/join or clarify acquisition. A blocked route is an access finding; preserve its potential value and follow-up.

## Qualify sources for specific claims

For every retained source or holder, state **how it advances the objective**: generates targets/areas, refines a prediction, disqualifies an option, supplies a parameter, verifies a claim or opens an acquisition route. Name what it cannot establish. Retain rejected leads with reasons in the process record.

Assess independently:

| Dimension | Required judgment |
| --- | --- |
| Existence | Confirmed, reported by an identified reference, or hypothesized; cite the basis. |
| Usefulness | Verified on a sample, plausibly useful, or unsuitable for a stated role. A description of a database is not acquired data. |
| Quality and trust | Relevant authority/expertise, accountable authorship, original evidence/method, vintage, incentives, limitations and corroboration. Qualify the specific claim. |
| Access | Known route and present status: public, paid, proprietary/permissioned, in-person, interview/introduction, unknown or unavailable. |
| Acquisition | Named custodian/organization and relevant role, records to request, supported procedure, prerequisites, known cost/effort and remaining questions. |

Prefer a relevant researcher's original work over anonymous commentary on that research; prefer an authoritative government record over unsupported discussion of that record. Authority remains claim-specific: an announcement does not prove delivery, an expert opinion is not a measurement, and an official record can be stale or outside its jurisdiction. Weak commentary can be a lead to original evidence without supporting the conclusion itself.

For human sources, distinguish records they may hold from opinions or recollections. Avoid inventing names, contents, procedures or prices. Check units, definitions, time, coverage and entity/spatial joins before combining observations. Multiple copies of one claim are not independent corroboration.

## Demonstrate usefulness and choose the next check

Try an early worked example against the stage's decision standard. Use original observations, supporting inference, adverse evidence and unresolved questions to show whether the source route actually produces a useful output. Missing verification is not automatically a negative screening result; circumstantial evidence is not automatically sufficient for every kind of research.

After the example, identify the weakest decision-critical link. Prioritize the next search by its plausible ability to change the decision, reduce a consequential uncertainty or improve many future outputs, weighed against acquisition effort. Explain this choice briefly; do not manufacture probabilities or information scores. Prefer reusable enrichment over repeated bespoke investigation when both address the same gap.

Present a short acquisition menu that includes valuable difficult routes as well as accessible ones: expected benefit, holder/product/office, next action, known cost/effort and unresolved access. Discovery does not itself authorize purchases or outreach.

Keep source coverage, acquired evidence, screening confidence and verified outcomes separate. Report what is established, what remains uncertain and the next useful action. Use the exploration's acceptance rules and host project's round closeout instructions to review saturation and readiness; workflow-only changes add no acquired evidence.

## Independent goal review

Use a separate reviewer for a discovery round's goal-achievement judgment, such as a subagent or a separate evaluation invocation. Provide the refined question, explicit requirements, inferred criteria with their rationale, original evidence and before/after state. Ask it to attempt an answer, check joins and contradictions, assess material gain, identify blockers and propose queue changes. Avoid supplying a desired verdict or only the worker's persuasive summary.

The reviewer must check both overstatement and excessive verification demands: judge sufficiency for the current decision stage. It may challenge inferred criteria with a reasoned revision proposal; record changes explicitly instead of silently easing the goal to declare success. Separate review does not establish real-world accuracy; retain disagreements and unresolved evidence.

## Capture methods during the work

After one example meets the agreed stage's success criterion, develop or update an **exploration-local skill as part of the work**, without waiting for another user prompt. Read [local method capture](local-method-capture.md) when doing so. Capture demonstrated procedures and limitations, then improve them through subsequent cases. A successful screen is sufficient to document a screening method; it does not establish its real-world predictive accuracy.


Host project records and recording utilities are integration inputs. For a standalone assignment, retain the approved brief, source provenance, observations, review artifacts and state-helper ledger locally; do not require absent repository files or create competing canonical registers.
