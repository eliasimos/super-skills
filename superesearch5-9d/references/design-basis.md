# Design basis and limits

Maintenance provenance only; not a research-stage read. Do not preload development results, benchmark rubrics or prior answers into a researcher's context.

## Lineage and controlled change

Created on 20 October 2026 directly from 5.9b, preserving that version and 5.9c. The source-selection method, answer map, required/supporting distinction, complete relevant figure extraction, interpretations, operational recommendations with bands and fallbacks, definitions, source verification, user approvals and invocation-selected model/effort are retained. The research-method and workspace-integration references are byte-identical to 5.9b.

Two focused changes are tested:
- Replace the mandatory per-obligation opening summary and closing synthesis with one substantive body and an optional short abstract. A distinct cross-topic inference still has a home in the body; it must not be lost with the recap. Source discussion supplies provenance or source evaluation without repeating the answer. The existing editorial pass follows this layout; no new pass or ledger is added.
- Restore the original body/abstract, inference and register instructions alongside the approved question before composition after compaction. Check actual time against the host deadline; do not treat a handoff's invented urgency as a new constraint.

## Observations motivating the experiment

In the latest two-question comparison, 5.9c used less research time and money than 5.9b but scored lower. Native command traces showed early skeletons followed by late large body writes, without intervening body edits. The intended evolving-report mechanism was not substantially executed. The host also required a working note. Consequently the run is not evidence that continuous composition itself failed or produced the savings.

The losses included unsupported speculative reconciliation, repeated conclusions and a missing interpretation of available evidence. One substantial presentation penalty concerned a contraction inside a quotation, making part of the score change brittle. A compaction handoff also introduced immediate writing urgency and a one-batch verification restriction despite substantial remaining time. These are observed failure modes, not established causal estimates.

The 5.9c revision changed more than its central construction mechanism: it condensed explicit writing constraints and removed process structures at the same time. This version returns to the 5.9b procedure to avoid carrying those confounds forward.

## Exclusions and limits

No new search-selection policy, weakest-dimension search, evolving-report requirement, team, index, reviewer, model mix, benchmark procedure or additional acquisition allowance is introduced. Broader literature-based gap discovery is a separate hypothesis.

The optional abstract rule addresses redundant presentation; it does not permit removal of required results, evidence, interpretations or adverse qualifications. The recovery safeguard restores existing instructions; it is not a second factual audit. Ordinary quotations must remain faithful to their sources; there is no special rewrite rule targeting a grader's treatment of contractions.

Expected observations are less repeated body content, fewer speculative reconciliations and less instruction drift after compaction. Lower output volume may reduce cost, but savings and quality improvements remain unproven. Single runs, live retrieval variation, strict rubric conventions and prior-answer exposure qualifications limit comparisons. Operational instructions contain no benchmark answers, named rubric targets or preferred benchmark sources.

No benchmark or new grading was run to create this skill. Structural validation does not establish behavioral compliance or a release candidate.
